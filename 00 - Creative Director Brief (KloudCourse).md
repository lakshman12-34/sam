# Creative Director Brief — KloudCourse Academy

Reference brief for all ad scripts in this folder.

## Brand
- **Brand:** KloudCourse Academy (EdTech / online learning)
- **Tagline:** Learn the Skills. Build the Future.
- **Colours:** Blue + Dark Purple
- **Positioning:** Relevant technical skills through structured, practical, industry-oriented learning.

## Audience
College students, final-year students, recent graduates, freshers, aspiring IT professionals, upskillers.

## Offer (only claim these)
- Courses: AI & ML, Data Science & Analytics, Full Stack, Cloud & DevOps, Cybersecurity, other emerging tech
- Structured modules, interactive quizzes & flashcards, practical assignments, certification-focused learning
- No stats, job guarantees, discounts or pricing unless provided.

## Philosophy
ATTENTION → CURIOSITY → CONNECTION → VALUE → BRAND → ACTION
- Open on reality, not promotion. Win the first 3 seconds.
- Brand appears late, as the story's natural answer.
- Sell value, not feature lists. Memorable ending.
- No "Hello everyone", no generic motivation, no laptop-in-every-scene.

## Every script delivers
1. Concept & title  2. Objective  3. Audience  4. 3–5 different hooks  5. Recommended hook + why
6. Telugu–English script (Telugu in Telugu script)  7. Scene-by-scene with timestamps
8. Visual/camera direction  9. Music & SFX  10. Brand reveal & CTA
Default length: 45–60 s.

## Final test
"Would a college student actually stop scrolling to watch this?" If no — rethink.

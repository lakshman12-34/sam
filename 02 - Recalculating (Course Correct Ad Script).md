# Campaign 02 — "RECALCULATING…"
### Campaign line: **Course Correct చేసుకో.**

**Format:** Reels / Shorts / YouTube pre-roll · 9:16 (16:9 safe) · **58 seconds**
**Language:** Telugu–English mix · burned-in captions
**Brand:** KloudCourse Academy. "Course Correct" is used as the campaign idea and line. *(If CourseCorrect is a separate brand, only the logo card and end super change.)*

---

## 1. Concept
Every student knows one voice better than their teachers: the **navigation app voice**. Calm, polite, never judging, and always saying *"Recalculating…"*

This ad turns that voice into the **narrator of a student's career**. Teja isn't failing. He's doing what most students do: **driving in circles**. A new trend every month, a new YouTube roadmap, a new "this is the future" post, and every time, *Recalculating.*

The twist: the GPS finally loses patience. Then Teja does what no one in his life told him to do. **He picks a destination.** That's when the map becomes a clear route of modules, quiz checkpoints, flashcard stops and assignment milestones, all the way to certification.

**Why it's different from "Same Resume":** that ad was a recruiter's harsh truth. This one is **comedy first**. It's warm, self-aware and shareable ("this is literally me"). The product is shown through the map UI, so features feel like a journey, not a list.

## 2. Primary objective
**Purchase intent.** Move the student from "I'll figure it out myself" to "I need a route", and make the KloudCourse learning path feel like the obvious next tap.

## 3. Target audience
Final-year students, recent grads and freshers (20–25) who have *started* learning many things and *finished* none: Python for two weeks, then Web Dev, then "AI is the future", then back to DSA.

**Psychological trigger:** recognition through humour, then the relief of direction. The insight is that they don't lack effort, they lack a route. That's flattering, not shaming, which makes people share it.

### Recommended: Hook 1
- **The sound itself is the hook.** "Recalculating" is a sound every phone user has heard thousands of times, so the brain snaps to attention before the visual even starts.
- **A visual punchline in 3 seconds.** The drone reveal of him looping a roundabout is funny, readable on mute and screenshot-worthy.
- **It owns the metaphor.** The whole ad (and the line "Course Correct") grows from this first image, so the brand payoff feels inevitable, not tacked on.
- Hooks 3 and 4 aren't wasted: they become the **"why he's circling"** montage inside the story.
- Hooks 2 and 5 work as standalone 15-second cutdowns in the same series.

## 5. Full script (Telugu–English)

> **NAV VOICE** *(calm, polite, slightly formal Telugu, like a navigation app)*:

> Destination enter చేయలేదు కదా, sir.

>

>
> **VO:**
> Course Correct చేసుకో.

## 9. Music & sound design
- **Signature sound:** the navigation chime plus "Recalculating". Design an **original** chime (don't copy any real app's sound) and reuse it as KloudCourse's sonic logo across the series.
- **0–8 s:** no score. Voice, night city ambience, a honk.
- **8–20 s:** playful pizzicato with tyre-squeak U-turns. It should feel like a comedy sketch.
- **20–34 s:** music drops out. The flyover echo, the cursor tick and one piano note carry the emotional turn.
- **34–54 s:** a warm electronic build, with a "ding" on each pin, peaking on the roundabout exit.
- **End:** the nav chime resolves into the brand sting.

## 10. Brand reveal & CTA
- The brand appears **only at 0:54**. Until then the product is "the route", so viewers want it before they know whose it is.
- The logo is **drawn by the route line itself**: the journey becomes the brand.
- **CTA:** "Your route starts here", with a "Start Learning" button styled like a nav app's Start button. It's familiar and tappable, which makes it ideal for paid social and the end-card link.
- No pricing or discounts (none provided).

## Creative director notes
- **Why people will watch to the end:** the comedy (U-turns, "హా తెలుసు!", "Destination enter చేయలేదు కదా, sir") keeps them through the first 25 s; the open loop of "where is he going?" pulls them to the payoff; and "Next destination?" / "Loading…" gives a smile at the very end, which drives shares and replays.
- **Why they'll want to buy:** the ad sells the one thing scattered free content doesn't give, **a route**: structure, checkpoints that confirm understanding, revision, practice and a finish line. Every element is a real feature from the brief.
- **Claims check:** no jobs, salaries or stats promised. "Arrived" means *finishing the course and certification*, not getting hired. "2022" and "రెండు సంవత్సరాలుగా" are character comedy, not data.
- **Legal / brand safety:** use an **original** map UI and voice. No Google Maps, Apple Maps or Ola branding, sounds or UI copies.
- **Casting:** Teja needs great comic timing and a very "normal" look. The nav voice should be a **female voice in polite, slightly formal Telugu**. The contrast with Teja's casual Telugu is where the humour lives.
- **Weak spot to watch:** the 0:08–0:20 life-map section can get busy. Keep it to **3 U-turns maximum** and 1-second inserts, or the comedy rhythm breaks.
- **Series plan ("Course Correct" campaign):**
  - 15 s cutdown A: Hook 2, the dinner table ("దేనికి?"), ending on "Destination set చేసుకో."
  - 15 s cutdown B: Hook 5, the wedding uncle, ending on "Next time answer ready గా ఉంచుకో."
  - 6 s bumper: the drone roundabout loop plus "Recalculating…", then the logo. Pure brand recall.
